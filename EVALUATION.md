# VLQ-1 Evaluation Framework

This document describes evaluation areas QEVARO intends to investigate for VLQ-1.

It does **not** claim completed benchmark results.

Measured results should only be published after the corresponding evaluation has been completed and reviewed.

---

## Romanian-language quality

Evaluation planning includes:

- grammar
- grammatical agreement
- Romanian diacritics
- punctuation
- natural phrasing
- lexical choice
- formal Romanian
- conversational Romanian
- register consistency

---

## Instruction following

Evaluation planning includes:

- direct instructions
- multi-step instructions
- formatting constraints
- conflicting constraints
- ambiguous instructions
- instruction prioritization

---

## Factual reliability

Evaluation planning includes:

- factual question answering
- unsupported factual claims
- grounded responses
- correction behavior
- uncertainty acknowledgement
- resistance to false premises

---

## Calibration

Evaluation planning includes:

- confidence relative to correctness
- ambiguous questions
- incomplete information
- insufficient evidence
- uncertainty expression

---

## Reasoning consistency

Evaluation planning includes:

- multi-step tasks
- internal consistency
- contradiction avoidance
- revision after new evidence
- difficult-task behavior

---

## Long-form behavior

Evaluation planning includes:

- entity consistency
- contradiction detection
- sustained Romanian-language quality
- instruction retention
- topic continuity

---

## Mixed-language behavior

Evaluation planning includes:

- Romanian + English prompts
- technical terminology
- code-switching
- translation boundaries
- preservation of language intent

---

## Publication policy

QEVARO distinguishes between:

- planned evaluations
- evaluations in progress
- completed evaluations
- reviewed results

No number should be presented as a VLQ-1 benchmark result unless the underlying evaluation has actually been completed.
