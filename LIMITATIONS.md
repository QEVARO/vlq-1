# VLQ-1 Development Limitations

VLQ-1 is currently under active development.

This document identifies areas that remain under investigation. It is not a quantitative failure report.

## Areas under investigation

Development and evaluation work includes problems such as:

- unsupported factual claims
- inconsistent reasoning on difficult tasks
- poorly calibrated confidence
- ambiguity handling
- long-form inconsistency
- instruction-following failures
- variation across Romanian registers
- Romanian / English mixed-language behavior

## No implied measurements

The presence of an item in this document does not imply a measured failure rate.

Specific rates, benchmarks, or comparative results will only be published after corresponding evaluations have been completed and reviewed.
