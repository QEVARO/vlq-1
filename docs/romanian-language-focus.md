# Romanian-Language Focus

Romanian is the primary language focus of VLQ-1.

QEVARO intends to evaluate Romanian-language behavior beyond simple translation quality.

## Language quality

Relevant areas include:

- grammar
- grammatical agreement
- Romanian diacritics
- punctuation
- natural phrasing
- lexical choice

## Register

Evaluation may include:

- formal writing
- conversational Romanian
- technical Romanian
- context-appropriate language

## Mixed-language behavior

Romanian users frequently interact with English technical terminology.

Evaluation may therefore include:

- Romanian prompts containing English terminology
- controlled code-switching
- preservation of technical meaning
- unnecessary anglicization

## Reliability

Linguistic fluency is not equivalent to factual correctness.

Romanian-language quality and factual reliability are therefore treated as separate evaluation concerns.
